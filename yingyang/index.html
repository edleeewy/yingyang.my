The Union of
Ying & Yang

Jhi Ying is short, Edward is tall.

Jhi Ying is serious, Edward is comedic.

Jhi Ying is graceful, Edward is…
still figuring that part out.

We come from different worlds with different ways of seeing and doing things, but somehow we just fit — like Malaysia itself, where countless differences somehow come together to create something beautiful, colourful, and full of life.

We’ve learned that love, like this country we call home, is about celebrating differences. It’s about listening, understanding, and finding harmony in the mix. So here we are: Ying & Yang, opposite in many ways, yet perfectly in sync.

Come join us as we take our first steps into one shared life, one shared home, and one exciting adventure ahead!
